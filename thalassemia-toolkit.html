Model limits: classical Mendelian inheritance of simplified deletional α-genotypes and β⁺/β⁰ alleles. Not modelled: non-deletional α variants, α-globin triplications, co-inheritance of α- and β-thalassemia, structural variants (HbE, HbS, HbC), HbF-modifying loci, de novo events or uniparental disomy.
